SCRIPT 2: CSE Department Weekly Meeting (Follow-up)
=====================================================
Duration target: ~6 minutes spoken
Speakers: 4 (same as Script 1)
  - JAYASHREE (female, department chairperson)
  - RAMESH (male, senior professor)
  - MEERA (female, NLP professor)
  - PAVAN (male, lab coordinator)

Setup: Follow-up meeting two weeks later. Reviewing action items from 
the previous meeting. This demonstrates temporal knowledge evolution 
across meetings for the capstone demo.

---

JAYASHREE: Morning everyone. Let's review our action items from the 
last meeting. Ramesh, start with the syllabus submission.

RAMESH: Submitted to the Board of Studies on time. They reviewed it 
yesterday. Generally positive feedback. One concern though. They want 
the assessment to include a hands-on project component, not just a 
written exam.

JAYASHREE: That's a fair point. What are you thinking?

RAMESH: A mini project worth thirty percent. Students deploy a deep 
learning model to production. Maybe on AWS free tier or Google Cloud. 
It tests practical skills better than an exam.

MEERA: That's a good idea. My students keep complaining that exams 
don't reflect real work.

JAYASHREE: Approved. Revise the assessment breakdown and resubmit. 
When's the next BOS meeting?

RAMESH: Two weeks from now. I'll have the revision ready.

JAYASHREE: Good. Pavan, the lab upgrade.

PAVAN: Ma'am, there's a problem. The GPU prices have gone up 
significantly in the last two weeks. The Dell quote I had was valid 
for seven days only. New quotes are coming in at about one point 
one two lakhs per machine.

JAYASHREE: That's a thirty five percent increase. Total cost?

PAVAN: For four machines, it comes to four point five lakhs. About 
one and a half lakh over our approved budget.

RAMESH: Can we delay the elective launch by a semester?

JAYASHREE: No, we've already advertised it to students. Pavan, what 
are our options?

PAVAN: I see two. One, reduce the quantity. Buy two now at two point 
two lakhs, two more next quarter. Two, look at refurbished or academic 
edition workstations. Some vendors offer academic pricing at twenty 
percent off.

JAYASHREE: I prefer option one. Splitting the purchase. Students can 
share machines in the first run. Submit the revised proposal today. 
Approved for two point two lakhs for phase one procurement.

PAVAN: Will do. I'll also ask about academic pricing for phase two.

JAYASHREE: Good thinking. Now, Meera, the capstone extension.

MEERA: The extension has helped most teams. But five teams still 
haven't submitted Phase 1. The new deadline is December twenty second. 
Three of them told me they're still finalizing the problem statement.

JAYASHREE: That's not acceptable. The extension was two weeks, not 
unlimited. Who are these teams?

MEERA: Teams seven, twelve, fifteen, eighteen, and twenty three.

JAYASHREE: Send them a warning email today. If they don't submit by 
December twenty second, they fail Phase 1 and redo it next semester. 
No more extensions.

MEERA: Agreed. I'll send the notice this afternoon.

RAMESH: What about teams that have submitted already? Are they getting 
feedback?

MEERA: Yes. I'm reviewing eight submissions this week. Feedback goes 
out by Friday.

JAYASHREE: Pavan, the TCS drive update.

PAVAN: The TCS drive is done. Happened on the eighth as planned. 
Forty two students appeared. Eighteen cleared the technical round. 
Final results announced last Monday. Twelve offers extended.

RAMESH: Twelve offers out of forty two. That's a twenty eight percent 
conversion. Better than last year's twenty percent.

PAVAN: Correct. The mock interview sessions helped. Students who 
attended both sessions had a sixty percent pass rate in the technical 
round, compared to thirty five percent for those who didn't attend.

MEERA: We should make the mock sessions mandatory for all students 
then, not just TCS candidates.

JAYASHREE: Good proposal. Let me discuss with the placement committee. 
What's next on the recruitment calendar?

PAVAN: Infosys is confirmed for January fifteenth. Wipro for January 
twentieth. Accenture is still pending. They promised a date by next week.

JAYASHREE: Follow up with Accenture by Wednesday. If they don't 
confirm, we schedule someone else. Can't keep that slot open.

PAVAN: I'll call them tomorrow.

JAYASHREE: One more item. The department website hasn't been updated 
since last year. Faculty profiles are outdated. Course pages show 
old syllabi. This looks bad during admission season.

RAMESH: I can assign two of my student assistants to update the 
content. They're good at web stuff. Should take three weeks.

JAYASHREE: Please do. We need it ready before February when admission 
queries pick up. Meera, can you coordinate with them on the NLP and 
AI course pages?

MEERA: Yes. I'll give them the updated content by end of this week.

JAYASHREE: Great. Anything else? No? Let's wrap up. Next meeting 
February second. Thank you everyone.
