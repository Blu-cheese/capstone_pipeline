SCRIPT 1: CSE Department Weekly Meeting
=========================================
Duration target: ~5 minutes spoken
Speakers: 4
  - JAYASHREE (female, department chairperson)
  - RAMESH (male, senior professor)
  - MEERA (female, NLP professor)
  - PAVAN (male, lab coordinator)

Setup: Weekly CSE department meeting. Topics: new elective, lab upgrade, 
student complaints, upcoming placement drive.

---

JAYASHREE: Good morning everyone. Thanks for making it on short notice. 
We have four items on the agenda today. Let's get started with the new 
elective proposal from Ramesh.

RAMESH: Thank you, ma'am. So, I've drafted the syllabus for Applied Deep 
Learning. The course code will be UE24AM350A. I'm proposing it as a 
sixth semester elective with Machine Learning as the prerequisite.

MEERA: Ramesh, quick question. How does this overlap with my NLP course? 
We both cover transformers.

RAMESH: That's a fair concern. I was thinking we could split it. I'll 
focus on vision transformers and image generation. You keep the language 
modeling side. Would that work?

MEERA: Yes, that works. I'll update my syllabus to remove the vision 
parts and expand on attention mechanisms instead.

JAYASHREE: Good. That's settled. Ramesh, when can you submit the final 
syllabus to the Board of Studies?

RAMESH: I can have it ready by next Friday.

JAYASHREE: Perfect. Next item. Pavan, the lab situation.

PAVAN: So we have eight workstations in AI Lab 2. Only four have GPUs. 
For the new elective, we'll need at least four more GPU-enabled machines. 
I got quotes from three vendors. The best one is Dell at 75,000 per 
workstation. So about 3 lakhs total for the upgrade.

JAYASHREE: 3 lakhs. Can we get this approved in this financial year?

PAVAN: If we submit by month end, yes. The administration needs two 
weeks for processing.

JAYASHREE: Okay. Pavan, you coordinate with purchase. Get the PO raised 
by the twenty fifth. Ramesh, since this is for your course, work with 
Pavan on the specifications.

RAMESH: Will do.

JAYASHREE: Third item. Student complaints about the capstone workload. 
I've received emails from four teams saying the Phase 1 deadline is too 
tight. Thoughts?

MEERA: I've heard similar concerns. The twelve week timeline assumes 
teams already have their topics finalized, which isn't always the case.

RAMESH: What if we extended it to fourteen weeks? That gives them two 
more weeks for the literature survey phase.

JAYASHREE: That's reasonable. Any objections? No? Then it's decided. 
Phase 1 is now fourteen weeks. I'll notify Dr. Chetana, who handles 
the capstone coordination, and she can update the handbook.

PAVAN: What about teams already in progress? Does this apply to them 
retroactively?

JAYASHREE: Good point. Yes, current teams get the extension. Their new 
deadline is December twenty second instead of December eighth.

MEERA: I'll announce this in my Phase 1 class tomorrow.

JAYASHREE: Thank you. Last item. The TCS campus recruitment is confirmed 
for January eighth. Pavan, you're handling logistics?

PAVAN: Yes. Booking the seminar hall is done. Online assessment will 
use the new computer lab. We need about two hundred seats. I'm 
coordinating with the placement cell.

RAMESH: Are we running mock interviews beforehand?

PAVAN: Planning two sessions. One on December twentieth for aptitude 
and technical. Another on December twenty seventh for HR rounds. 
Both sessions are mandatory for students who registered.

JAYASHREE: Good. Make sure the mentor assignments are finalized by 
next week. Ramesh, you're mentoring batch A?

RAMESH: Yes. Twelve students. I've already met them once.

JAYASHREE: Excellent. Any other business? No? Then we're done. 
Same time next week. Thank you everyone.
